DSC 106 Project 3 – GOES Satellite Visualization Checkpoint

Research direction: How can GOES satellite thermal infrared imagery reveal atmospheric patterns relevant to tracking weather systems? This checkpoint explores one hour of GOES-19 Channel 13 (10.3 µm) data as early pipeline progress toward the final interactive visualization. Each frame is a 5-minute scan; brighter pixels = colder (higher) cloud tops.
